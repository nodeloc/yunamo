# Yunamo 使用规则

你需要对通过子域名发布的内容和发送的邮件负责。个人博客、作品集、文档、项目演示和学习用途都受欢迎。完整规则见官网。[使用规范](https://yunamo.com/acceptable-use) · [服务条款](https://yunamo.com/pages/terms-of-service)

- 禁止发布钓鱼页面、传播恶意软件、实施诈骗，或冒充他人及机构。
- 禁止发送未经请求的群发邮件、发起网络攻击，或扫描他人未授权你测试的系统。
- 禁止批量抢注、出售或出租平台子域名，或用域名发布违法内容、侵犯版权。
- 违规可能导致域名暂停或移除、账号封禁。发现滥用时，请通过官网表单提交域名、相关网址和具体情况。对处置有异议，可在收到通知后 14 天内发邮件申诉。[举报滥用](https://yunamo.com/report) · [联系支持](mailto:support@yunamo.com)

## English

You are responsible for what you publish and send through your subdomain. Personal sites, portfolios, documentation, demos and learning projects are welcome. The full policies are on the website. [Acceptable use policy](https://yunamo.com/acceptable-use) · [Terms of service](https://yunamo.com/pages/terms-of-service)

- Phishing, malware, scams and impersonation are prohibited.
- Unsolicited bulk email, network attacks and unauthorized scanning are prohibited.
- Do not mass-register, sell or rent out subdomains, distribute illegal content or infringe copyright.
- Violations can lead to domain suspension or removal and account bans. To report abuse, submit the domain, relevant URLs and a description using the form. To appeal, email support within 14 days of the enforcement notice. [Report abuse](https://yunamo.com/report) · [Contact support](mailto:support@yunamo.com)
